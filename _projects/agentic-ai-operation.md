Studied and implemented AI Agents to accelerate DevOps and SRE team operations, with the following main focus areas:
- SRE Troubleshoot: Agent to perform pre-assessment of alerts sent to the team, analyzing logs, metrics, and traces. Reduced team escalations.
- Task Refinement: Provided additional context and information for tasks to be executed by the team, improving activity planning, reducing delivery time, and better aligning requirements with stakeholders.
- Management Reports: Daily operations, projects, and delivery performance reports. Improved visibility and insights into team performance and operational improvement areas.
- Auto Code Review: Specialized agents for code review focusing on different areas (cost, performance, security, and best practices), elevating delivery quality and reducing rework and review time by other collaborators.
Key Outcomes:
- Reduced alert escalations to on-call teams
- Improved delivery planning and time-to-market
- Better operational visibility and insights
- Elevated code quality and reduced review cycles
